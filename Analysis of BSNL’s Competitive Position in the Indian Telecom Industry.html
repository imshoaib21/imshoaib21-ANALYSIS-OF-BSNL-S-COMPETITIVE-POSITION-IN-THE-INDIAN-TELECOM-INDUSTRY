Abstract

India’s telecom industry has changed a lot in the last few years because of the rapid growth of mobile internet, 4G and 5G services. Today, the market is mainly controlled by a few large private telecom companies. BSNL was once one of the leading telecom service providers in India, but over time it has lost a significant part of its wireless market share. Strong competition, slow technology upgrades and changing customer needs are some of the major reasons behind this decline.

This project focuses on understanding the current competitive position of BSNL in the Indian telecom industry with the help of business analytics. The study is based completely on secondary data collected from reliable sources such as TRAI reports, government publications, BSNL releases, Parliamentary documents and other published studies. Simple analytical methods such as trend analysis, market share analysis, subscriber analysis, competitor comparison, circle-wise comparison and SWOT analysis have been used.

The study shows that BSNL has faced a decline in its subscriber base and market share over the years. Private telecom companies moved faster in adopting 4G and 5G technologies, while BSNL was comparatively late in upgrading its network. However, recent data also shows some improvement in its subscriber base and financial performance.

Overall, BSNL still has some important strengths such as government support, strong presence in rural areas, wide network reach and fiber infrastructure. For better future growth, BSNL needs to focus on faster technology upgrades, improved network quality, better customer service and effective use of business analytics for understanding and retaining customers.

Introduction

  • In India, communication has become an essential aspect of daily life. It supports digital payments, online education, healthcare, government services, business operations, and personal communication.
  • Indian telecom has undergone major shifts from fixed-line services to 2G, 3G, 4G, and 5G. As of March 2026, India had over 1.28 billion wireless access subscriptions, as reported by TRAI.
  • Reliance Jio, Bharti Airtel, Vodafone Idea, and BSNL are the major telecom operators.
  • BSNL was established in 2000 after service-related activities of the Department of Telecommunications were transformed into a separate company. It provides mobile, landline, broadband, enterprise, government, rural and remote connectivity services.
  • BSNL launched 2G mobile services in 2002 and 3G services in 2009.
  • Reliance Jio’s entry in September 2016 transformed the market through 4G-based services, competitive pricing, and increased data usage.
  • With the launch of 5G in India in October 2022, technology and network investment became even more important.
  • Private operators outpaced BSNL’s 4G rollout, while government revival packages were introduced to support BSNL’s 4G and 5G expansion and overall revival.

Objective of the Study

2.1 Statement of the Problem

BSNL has faced a major decline in its competitive position in the wireless telecom market, especially during the period when mobile data became the main source of competition. Its wireless subscriber base declined from about 113.74 million in March 2022 to about 88.25 million in March 2024, before improving to nearly 92.94 million by March 2026. Its March 2026 market share was 7.25%, compared with 39.71% for Reliance Jio and 37.55% for Bharti Airtel.

Slower adoption of new technology, especially 4G and 5G, affected BSNL’s ability to deliver high-speed data services. BSNL also faced financial pressure, although income improved and annual net loss declined over time. Performance also varied across telecom circles.

2.2 Objectives of the Study

  1. To study the trend of BSNL’s wireless subscriber base.
  2. To compare BSNL’s market share with Jio, Airtel and Vodafone Idea.
  3. To examine BSNL’s recent financial and operational performance.
  4. To compare BSNL’s performance across selected telecom circles.
  5. To understand the impact of delayed 4G and 5G adoption.
  6. To identify BSNL’s major strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats.
  7. To suggest practical, data-based strategies for improving competitiveness and customer retention.

2.3 Scope of the Study

The study focuses mainly on Indian wireless telecom services, with BSNL as the main company and Jio, Airtel and Vodafone Idea as competitors. Subscriber and market-share trends cover March 2022 to March 2026. Financial analysis mainly covers FY2020-21 to FY2024-25. The study uses secondary data only.

2.4 Contribution of this Project

The project combines subscriber trends, market share, financial performance, technology adoption and telecom-circle analysis in one study. It also shows that subscriber growth does not always translate into market-share growth when competitors are growing faster.

Review of Literature

3.1 Competition, Market Structure and the “Jio Effect”

Parsheera and Trehan (2022) found that the entry of Reliance Jio reduced the number of active operators, lowered data prices and increased market concentration. Mathews and Sivaraman (2025) highlighted the importance of technology adoption and regulation in shaping telecom market share. Kumar and Bose (2022) discussed management limitations, financial pressure, changing customer expectations and the need for stronger business strategies at BSNL and MTNL.

3.2 Customer Switching and Technology Adoption

Dhankhar and Dasgupta (2023) found that customers compare operators on price, service quality and value-added services. This supports the view that BSNL’s subscriber loss can also be understood as a customer-retention issue.

3.3 Revival Policy and Financial Performance

Government revival packages supported spectrum allocation, debt restructuring, rural connectivity and network expansion. BSNL’s total income improved and losses declined over the FY2020-21 to FY2024-25 period.

3.4 Network Modernisation and Research Gap

By 28 February 2026, 97,906 indigenous 4G sites had been installed and 96,103 were operational. The report identifies a research gap in combining subscriber trends, market share, financial performance, technology development and circle-wise analysis using recent data.

Results and Discussion

6.1 Findings of the Study

  • BSNL’s March 2026 wireless market share was 7.25%, far below Jio and Airtel.
  • Its subscriber base fell sharply between 2022 and 2024 before recovering to about 92.94 million by March 2026.
  • Its market share also declined from about 9.96% in March 2022 to 7.25% in March 2026.
  • Delayed 4G rollout was a major competitive weakness.
  • More than 96,000 4G sites were operational by February 2026.
  • Financial performance improved, with higher income and lower losses.
  • Regional performance varies significantly across telecom circles.

6.2 Suggestions

  • Use the growing 4G network to improve customer experience.
  • Monitor network speed, coverage, complaints, site utilisation and customer additions at circle level.
  • Prepare a clear, time-based 5G expansion plan.
  • Use business analytics to identify customers likely to churn.
  • Use different strategies for stronger and weaker telecom circles.
  • Improve the mobile app, digital support and complaint-resolution process.
  • Bundle mobile services with Bharat Fibre, FTTH, enterprise and government connectivity.

Conclusion

BSNL has played an important role in India’s telecommunications development, especially in rural connectivity, landline services, government networks and public communication. However, the rise of mobile data, 4G and 5G and strong competition from Jio and Airtel created major challenges.

The study shows that BSNL’s wireless subscribers and market share declined significantly, although subscribers recovered to around 92.94 million by March 2026. Its market share remained at only 7.25%.

The delayed nationwide 4G expansion was one of the main reasons behind its weaker position. Still, activation of more than 96,000 4G sites by February 2026 represents meaningful progress. Financially, BSNL also improved, with higher income, lower losses and profits in two consecutive quarters during FY2024-25.

Overall, BSNL’s revival has started but is not complete. Future competitiveness will depend on faster technology implementation, better network quality, improved customer service and stronger use of business analytics.
